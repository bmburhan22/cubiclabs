## Specifications

### Required:

- **Dimensions**: 1920x1080 pixels (16:9 aspect ratio)
- **File Size**: Under 2MB (for best performance)
- **Quality**: High resolution, sharp and clear

### What to Screenshot:
Take a screenshot of one of these:
1. **Your actual website homepage** (best option!)
2. **Your dashboard/app interface**
3. **A mockup of your product**
4. **Your portfolio/work samples**

## How to Add Your Screenshot

### Method 1: Screenshot Your Current Site
1. Open your website in a browser
2. Set browser window to 1920x1080 (full HD)
3. Take a screenshot (use a tool like Snagit, Lightshot, or built-in screenshot)
4. Save as `website.png`
5. Place in: `C:\Users\USER\burhan\cubiclabs\public\images\website.png`

### Method 2: Use a Design Tool
1. Create a design in Figma/Photoshop
2. Canvas size: 1920x1080px
3. Design your website layout
4. Export as PNG
5. Save as `website.png` in the images folder

### Method 3: Use a Screenshot Service
1. Go to https://www.screely.com/ or https://screenshot.rocks/
2. Upload your site URL
3. Download the screenshot (1920x1080)
4. Rename to `website.png`
5. Place in the images folder

---

## Quick Tips

✅ **Good Screenshots:**
- Shows your actual UI/product
- Clean, professional looking
- Good contrast and readable text
- Shows your brand/colors
- Highlights key features

❌ **Avoid:**
- Blurry or pixelated images
- Screenshots with personal info
- Low resolution images
- Wrong aspect ratio (will stretch)
- Screenshots with Lorem ipsum text
